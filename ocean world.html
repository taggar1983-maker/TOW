The ocean world 🐙

why we should help the ocean 🐡

Helping the ocean is essential because it produces over half of Earth’s oxygen, regulates global climate, supports millions of species, and provides food, jobs, medicine, and economic resources for people worldwide. Oxygen production: The ocean generates more than half of the world’s oxygen through phytoplankton. Economic value: Ocean-related industries generate billions of dollars and support millions of jobs. Medicine: Many medical compounds, including those used to treat cancer and heart disease, Cultural and recreational importance: Oceans offer recreation, tourism, and inspiration for communities worldwide. Food supply: The ocean provides at least one-fifth of the animal protein people eat globally. Biodiversity: Over two million species may live in the ocean, many still undiscovered. Fish facts 🐬

Fish are incredibly diverse vertebrates—over 20,000 to 34,000 species exist—and include everything from tiny gobies under half an inch to whale sharks over 50 feet long. There are roughly 34,000–37,000 recognized fish species worldwide, with current authoritative counts ranging from about 34,800 (FishBase, 2022) to 37,678 (Eschmeyer’s Catalog, 2026).
